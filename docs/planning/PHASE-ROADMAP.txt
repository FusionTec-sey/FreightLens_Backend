SHARED UI CONTRACT, INCLUDING T33A
Reuse FreightLens branding and shared layout primitives; no replacement catalogue,
cart, manager-case engine or permission system. Register -> detail -> explicit
action is the common pattern. Desktop may keep the register beside detail;
tablet prioritises the active task with large controls and contained scrolling.
Keep company/store context, clear loading/error/permission states, retained save
results and safe uncertain retries. No KPI cards on operational work screens.
Navigation and tabs are read-only; entering a view must never post a business effect.

T33A adapts the integrated count work: count register/filters/status, desktop
detail and tablet blind-entry layout. Its task-specific controls are Submit count,
Request recount and Review, not sales/cart/payment controls. Expected stock,
prior counts and financial values remain absent from blind-counter responses
and views; manager-only discrepancy details remain separately authorised.
No stock adjustment, inventory freeze or automatic reconciliation is added.
Shared navigation/component edits require integrator coordination. Backend/count
work can continue independently of sales checkout. Visual direction is approved;
no collaborator implementation or acceptance status is inferred from that approval.
