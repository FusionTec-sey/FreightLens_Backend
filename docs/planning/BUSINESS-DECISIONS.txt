BD-20261003-01 | Operating scope and commercial rules | T03,T06,T11,T12,T20,T28,T30
One operating company; separate branch accounts/licences do not imply separate
legal operating companies. Locations remain configurable, not hard-coded tenants.
Use explicit valuation pools and independent selling-store price lists. Island
distribution costs may differ; no automatic price change from a cost posting.
SCR selling prices are tax-inclusive; VAT15% is the owner's starting requirement,
not a hard-coded universal rate. Model exemptions/additional taxes configurably.
Imported currencies include USD, ZAR and GBP with recorded conversion evidence.
Actual tax/accounting configuration still needs Seychelles-specific validation.
Best eligible customer price must respect agreed exclusive terms and approval
floors. Customer credit never expires. Missing receiving-account mappings block
posting; never select another store's account. Credit-limit exceptions need exact
approval. No guessed bank mappings, operational thresholds or opening balances.
Trading calendars support Saturday and explicit public-holiday exceptions.

BD-20261003-02 | Reservations and fulfilment | T07,T13,T16,T27,T29
Planning, reservation and physical handover are distinct. Sales staff agree
reservation/follow-up dates with the customer; expiry starts review, never silent
cancellation, refund, credit conversion or reassignment of paid holds.
Confirmed orders need a qualifying deposit or agreed credit terms. Payment and
fulfilment statuses remain separate. Tiles retain required matching batch/shade/
calibre rules. Explicit special requests/approvals govern other-store fulfilment.
Returns must be verified against original invoice lines and actual handovers,
including previous/pending returns; no unsupported return or automatic balance merge.
Manager exceptions bind exact action, source versions and quantity and are audited.

BD-20261003-03 | Default salesperson work area | T03,T07,T14
Ordinary eligible allocation is automatic within the salesperson's configured
working area. Insufficient local stock shows a shortfall, not automatic spillover.
Other areas may be selected explicitly when needed; applicable multi-location,
reservation and management approval rules remain mandatory.
Supersedes any interpretation that every ordinary local allocation needs a manual
location choice or that a shortfall permits automatic other-store allocation.
Implemented so far: optional counter default_stock_location_id, active scoped
validation, versioned resolver and settings picker. Staff assignment, area-descendant
planning and automatic posting are NOT implemented by that configuration alone.

BD-20261005-01 | Accelerated three-lane cloud sales pilot | T08-T20
Owner approved a pilot-first parallel delivery plan on 2026-10-05. Establish one
paired, verified backend/frontend baseline before creating feature worktrees. Then
run three active lanes: integrator/core, full-stack programmer and checkout UI.
The integrator exclusively owns shared stock/posting/authority contracts, common
registries, permission catalogues, migration ordering and final merges. The
programmer starts with T12A, then remaining T10 acceptance and T17. The checkout
lane finishes T14B-D/T15 and later connects T14/T16/T18 to frozen backend contracts.

Use one task branch per bounded package and paired backend/frontend PRs when both
repositories change. No feature branch starts from an uncommitted checkout. Reserve
migration filenames before implementation; other lanes supply registration manifests
instead of editing protected registration files. Run focused checks per PR and full
backend/frontend/fresh-install gates at the paired baseline, atomic-posting checkpoint
and pilot acceptance. This supersedes serial execution as the current scheduling
policy but does not relax business rules, dependency gates or release authority.

The first supervised cloud pilot prioritises customer selection, configured store
pricing/tax, protected reservations, configured cash and externally confirmed card
methods, atomic sale posting, invoices, line-level collection, returns/credit notes
and till close. Cheques, deposits, contract credit, promotions, full reporting,
provider delivery and T21-T24 offline work remain later. No deployment, real account
mapping, live financial posting or customer messaging is authorised by this decision.

BD-20261005-04 | Manual till count; accounting module is future scope | T20,T26,T32,T34
Owner decided on 2026-10-05 that cash counting is a manual procedure performed by
the cashier at reception. No till reconciliation, opening-float, blind-count or
cash-deposit feature is required in the software for the current delivery.

The accounting module is explicitly FUTURE scope: it will be developed later, so
it is retained in the plan and not cancelled. Do not expand accounting work now.
The T20A foundation already implemented (versioned per-branch logical account
mappings and exact balanced journal composition) STAYS IN PLACE exactly as built:
fail-closed, no real account seed, no cross-branch fallback and no financial
activation. Do not remove or weaken it, and do not seed real accounts or activate
posting against it.

Current T20 scope therefore reduces to that inert foundation. Till reconciliation,
discrepancy approval, deposit matching and journal export move to the future
accounting module together with their own accounting, provider and owner gates.

Downstream tasks must not wait for the future accounting module:
- T26 import and recovery rehearsals keep import, opening reconciliation and
  backup restore; accounting export rehearsal is out of scope while deferred.
- T32 reporting proceeds on sales, stock and customer data; accounting journals
  and ledger exports are excluded until the future module exists.
- T34 pilot gates drop till reconciliation and accounting export acceptance.
  Cash handling is covered by the manual reception procedure, which staff
  acceptance should still cover as an operational step.

CHANGE PROTOCOL
Append dated stable IDs with affected tasks and superseded decisions. Only record
agreed requirements; mark unresolved proposals explicitly. Publish to integration
through a reviewed change and notify affected work on its shared task/PR. Both
developers fetch/check decisions before tasks and PR/merge; invalidated work must
be revised before merging. A chat message alone does not update another developer.
PRs state decision revision, companion PR, tests and outstanding release gates.
