BD-20261003-01 | Operating scope and commercial rules | T03,T06,T11,T12,T20,T28,T30
One operating company; separate branch accounts/licences do not imply separate
legal operating companies. Locations remain configurable, not hard-coded tenants.
Use explicit valuation pools and independent selling-store price lists. Island
distribution costs may differ; no automatic price change from a cost posting.
SCR selling prices are tax-inclusive; VAT15% is the owner's starting requirement,
not a hard-coded universal rate. Model exemptions/additional taxes configurably.
Imported currencies include USD, ZAR and GBP with recorded conversion evidence.
Actual tax/accounting configuration still needs Seychelles-specific validation.
Best eligible customer price must respect agreed exclusive terms and approval
floors. Customer credit never expires. Missing receiving-account mappings block
posting; never select another store's account. Credit-limit exceptions need exact
approval. No guessed bank mappings, operational thresholds or opening balances.
Trading calendars support Saturday and explicit public-holiday exceptions.

BD-20261003-02 | Reservations and fulfilment | T07,T13,T16,T27,T29
Planning, reservation and physical handover are distinct. Sales staff agree
reservation/follow-up dates with the customer; expiry starts review, never silent
cancellation, refund, credit conversion or reassignment of paid holds.
Confirmed orders need a qualifying deposit or agreed credit terms. Payment and
fulfilment statuses remain separate. Tiles retain required matching batch/shade/
calibre rules. Explicit special requests/approvals govern other-store fulfilment.
Returns must be verified against original invoice lines and actual handovers,
including previous/pending returns; no unsupported return or automatic balance merge.
Manager exceptions bind exact action, source versions and quantity and are audited.

BD-20261003-03 | Default salesperson work area | T03,T07,T14
Ordinary eligible allocation is automatic within the salesperson's configured
working area. Insufficient local stock shows a shortfall, not automatic spillover.
Other areas may be selected explicitly when needed; applicable multi-location,
reservation and management approval rules remain mandatory.
Supersedes any interpretation that every ordinary local allocation needs a manual
location choice or that a shortfall permits automatic other-store allocation.
Implemented so far: optional counter default_stock_location_id, active scoped
validation, versioned resolver and settings picker. Staff assignment, area-descendant
planning and automatic posting are NOT implemented by that configuration alone.

BD-20261003-06 | Store-wide selling; counter is a preference | T03,T07,T14
Supersedes BD-20261003-03's hard counter-area boundary. Staff may sell any eligible
product in their authorised InventoryBranch, regardless of counter/specialism.
The usual counter remains a workstation, separate from staff/store permissions.
default_stock_location_id is retained as an optional preferred picking area, not
an entitlement. Unset means no preference, not blocked sales. Prefer eligible stock
in that area, then other compatible locations in the same branch before shortage.
Store-wide never means every warehouse on the island. Another store or separately
configured warehouse requires explicit choice and applicable reviewed approval;
no company-boundary exception. Preserve batch/shade/calibre, exact source-demand
caps, reservations and retries. Same-store compatible location splits are allowed.
Initial automatic allocation must not silently replace/top up historical holds;
their reviewed lifecycle remains protected. T33A remains collaborator-owned.
