FreightLens receipt cost public review checkpoint — 2026-10-04

Scope
- Added permission-separated public request/list/independent-review endpoints for exact receipt unit-price and SCR exchange-rate evidence.
- Added reviewed, version-pinned document downloads that revalidate the complete current binding before and after storage I/O.
- Added the Goods Receiving manifest UI for SCR or documented foreign-currency declarations, existing purchase-document selection, stable retries, separate reviewer views and version-bound decisions.
- Kept classification review, cost-evidence review and later physical/financial posting as distinct operations. Approval has no stock, valuation, selling-price or accounting effect.

Safety boundaries
- Financial and supplier field access is required in addition to the dedicated request/review permission.
- Generic manifest and case reads do not expose storage paths, object keys, hashes or version identities.
- Foreign currency requires a separate FX evidence document; SCR requires a rate of exactly 1.
- Self-review, stale source/document content, company mismatch and unknown storage-size configuration fail closed.
- Object storage is read outside the business transaction; current source and fingerprint binding are checked again under a fresh database transaction.
- Retry operation identities remain stable. Review decisions use the listed case version rather than a client default.
