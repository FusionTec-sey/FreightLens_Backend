FreightLens T11 exact pricing/tax calculation foundation - 2026-10-04

Implemented
- Pure typed service; no database, stock, invoice, payment or price mutation.
- Requires exactly one versioned selling-store price and accepts only explicitly
  eligible versioned customer-agreement prices. Lowest eligible gross price wins;
  equal-price ties retain the store source.
- Optional gross floor produces requires_floor_approval without altering the chosen
  customer price or creating an approval.
- Tax is an independent versioned STANDARD, ZERO_RATED or EXEMPT snapshot. Standard
  rate is positive/configurable; zero-rated and exempt remain distinct at rate zero.
- Tax-inclusive SCR decomposes into separate net and tax. The invoice gross is rounded
  once to cents, then line gross and tax cents are allocated deterministically so all
  displayed line and invoice totals conserve exactly.

Verification
- 16 focused tests pass, including standard 115/100/15 decomposition, zero/exempt
  distinction, agreement selection, floor flags, mixed tax groups, ambiguous input
  denial and 200 deterministic multi-line invoices.

Official basis checked 2026-10-04
- Seychelles Revenue Commission Tax System:
  https://src.gov.sc/seychelles-tax-system/
- SRC VAT Guideline:
  https://www.src.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/VAT-Guideline.pdf
- SRC materials state a current 15% standard rate, distinct zero-rated and exempt
  supplies, and separate VAT display on invoices/receipts. These are evidence for
  configurable defaults and invoice fields, not permission to hard-code product
  classifications or treat guidance as an exhaustive legal opinion.
