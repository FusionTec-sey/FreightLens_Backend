T08 AUTHORITATIVE PRODUCT QUANTITY PROJECTION — 2026-10-04

Outcome
- Product catalogue/list/detail/lookup/export responses now derive stock from
  tenant-scoped Inventory StockBalance rows, not Product.current_stock.
- On-hand, reserved, available, damaged and quarantined quantities remain separate
  exact six-decimal strings. current_stock is only a temporary response alias for
  the authoritative on-hand result; it never reads the legacy Product column.
- Products with more than one base unit return MIXED_UNITS and no aggregate rather
  than summing incompatible quantities. A true no-balance product remains an exact
  zero with NO_BALANCE status.
- Low-stock filters, statistics and dashboard counts use scoped StockBalance
  summaries and exclude mixed-unit comparisons.
- The product search document no longer publishes legacy current_stock. Search is
  still rehydrated through PostgreSQL and the exact Inventory projection.
- The dashboard valuation no longer multiplies Product.current_stock by catalogue
  unit cost. It sums the latest immutable cost-pool head per product/pool in SCR,
  labels the result provisional and exposes missing-valuation coverage.
- Product Master, quick view, selected CSV export, catalogue selector and legacy
  order picker use the authoritative fields. Unavailable/mixed totals are never
  rendered as zero or “Out of stock.” The selector explains preferred same-store
  allocation and explicit authorised other-store choice without inventing a
  central-warehouse assignment.

Boundaries
- No stock writer, adjustment, receipt, import, allocation or financial-posting
  action was enabled by this slice.
- The legacy database Product.current_stock column remains at zero for compatibility
  and for policy-transition safety checks until a reviewed migration removes it.
- T08 stays In progress: controlled reviewed location adjustments, authorised
  opening/import composition and the public receipt runtime/retention gates remain.
- Dashboard valuation remains UNRECONCILED/provisional; T20/T32 accounting and
  reporting gates are not implied.
- Browser acceptance was not run because the applicable Product Master/dashboard
  automation approval was not supplied for this slice.
