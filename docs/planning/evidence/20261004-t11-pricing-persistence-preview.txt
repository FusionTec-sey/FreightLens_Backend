T11 PRICING, REVIEWED FLOOR AND IMMUTABLE INPUT EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-10-04

Scope
-----
- Persisted stable tax-rule, branch-product-selling-unit price, product-tax and
  customer-branch-product-selling-unit agreement identities.
- Every change appends a consecutive immutable revision. Exact retries use one
  stable operation identity; changed intent and stale versions fail closed.
- Added tenant-scoped paginated API routes. SALES + View_Product + View_Financials
  are required for reads; Manage_Financials is required for writes. Customer
  agreements additionally require customer and personal-data access.
- Added an authoritative, read-only sales-draft preview. It resolves current
  configuration and returns exact tax-inclusive SCR net/tax/gross values, selected
  source and versions, and whether the customer-beneficial price is below floor.
- Added Sales > Pricing & tax with separate Tax rules, Store prices, Product tax
  and Customer prices registers, version-aware forms and paginated source pickers.
- Added reusable manager cases for below-floor prices, bound to the exact draft
  revision and canonical price/tax fingerprint. Requestors cannot self-review and
  a changed draft or pricing configuration invalidates the decision path.
- Added immutable prepared transaction-pricing inputs. The record retains exact
  source/configuration versions, line values, tax treatments and invoice totals,
  references the approved floor case when required, and is protected by stable
  retry identity and tenant scope.
- Added price-floor review and exact-pricing preparation to the sales frontend.
  Review access is independent from customer-facing draft access where the backend
  contract permits it; the draft screen clearly distinguishes preparation from sale.

Business and safety boundaries
------------------------------
- Tax classification remains separate from every price list.
- Selling unit is part of store-price and customer-agreement identity.
- No VAT rate, tax classification, store price, customer agreement or real business
  record is seeded. Configuration must be supplied through authorised workflows.
- Preview does not create an invoice, reserve or move stock, collect money, approve
  a floor exception or activate financial posting.
- Floor review records a decision only. Pricing preparation references but does not
  consume approval. T13 must consume the same exact case and snapshot atomically
  with the eventual invoice, stock, money, numbering and outbox effects.
- Product eligibility lookup avoids procurement/supplier cost relationships.
