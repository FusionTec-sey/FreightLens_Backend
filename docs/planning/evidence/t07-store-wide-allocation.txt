IMPLEMENTATION

  Missing preference yields null root/empty inspection, not an allocation error.
- Independent trusted BranchActionContext carries branch and counter; preference
  does not assign staff, grant product permissions or create node authority.
- Internal initial-allocation coordinator uses saved source/version, permission
  and persisted authority checks before replay. Reuses shared locked source loader.
- Product-scoped streamed candidates exclude foreign branches/companies, inactive
  ancestors, unavailable quantities and expired batches. Prefer counter descendants
  then compatible stock elsewhere in the same InventoryBranch.
- Same-batch/shade/calibre splits use deterministic child reserve operations and
  one atomic grouping receipt/outbox. A short preferred lot does not mask another
  sufficient eligible lot. Failure rolls back every child; no partial allocation.
- Existing hold history cannot be silently replaced/topped up. Source-linked
  splits enforce combined exact demand caps. Generic source-less splits and
  unapproved same-bucket supplements remain denied; SQL segment guards unchanged.
- No schema migration required. No second ledger, catalogue or approval engine.

  Explicit wording separates workstation/specialism, selling store and permissions.
