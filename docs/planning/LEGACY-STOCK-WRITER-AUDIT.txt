   ProductMasterPage no longer sends stock or provides an editable baseline field.

6. ProductCatalogSelector quick-create no longer sends current_stock. Its catalogue,

   fields and render incompatible totals unavailable rather than zero.

   a reader, bulk-category/delete are not stock imports. T26 must use Inventory.

submission and PO status/notification. It does not create stock balances/movements.

needs stable receipt-line identity, exact reviewed policy/unit conversion,
branch/location/batch/serial identity, node fencing and one shared transaction.
